PORTFOLIO THAT SAVES ONLINE, WORKS ON ANY DEVICE, AND ANYONE CAN VIEW

Files: index.html, style.css, script.js, config.js, setup.sql (keep them in one folder).

STEP 1  Free database
  Go to supabase.com, sign up, and click "New project". Wait until it is ready.

STEP 2  Set it up
  Open SQL Editor > New query. Paste everything from setup.sql and click Run.
  Open Authentication > Users > Add user. Enter YOUR email and a password.
  Open Authentication > Sign In / Providers and turn OFF "Allow new users to sign up"
  (this keeps everyone else from editing your portfolio).

STEP 3  Connect
  Open Project Settings > API. Copy the Project URL and the anon public key
  into config.js between the quotes and save.

STEP 4  Publish
  Upload the whole folder to Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) or GitHub Pages.
  You get one link. Open it on any phone or computer.

HOW TO USE
  Click "Owner login" and sign in. Now you can upload many pictures and files
  to each section, add your photo, and click your name/bio to edit. Everything
  saves online right away. Visitors who open the link see everything, view only.
  Any file type works (limit 50 MB per file on the free plan).
